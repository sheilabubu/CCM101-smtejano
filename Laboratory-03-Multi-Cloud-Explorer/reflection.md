## Mission Reflection

In completing this laboratory which is the Multi-Cloud Explorer, it gaves me a better understanding of how cloud computing platforms can support different types of organizations, like AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. AWS I like more because of this flexibility and extensive selection of cloud services. I learned that AWS can provide solutions for computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and other business requirements. AWS, Azure, and GCP are similar because this three can provide cloud-based services that organizations can use without having to manage all of their physical infrastructure. Also They offer services such as virtual machines, cloud storage, databases, networking, identity and access management, and Kubernetes and also support scalability, allowing businesses to increase or decrease their resources depending on their needs. Business requirements are also important when selecting a cloud platform. Different companies have different budgets, existing technologies, workloads, and future goals, for example organizations that already depend on Microsoft technologies may prefer Azure because of the compatibility with Microsoft products. Companies that focus heavily on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data may consider GCP, while AWS can be suitable for businesses looking for a broad selection of services. Also Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that cloud selection requires careful analysis, instead of choosing a provider based only on popularity, I need to consider the client's budget, current infrastructure, workload, security requirements, scalability, and long-term plans. This laboratory also improved my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio, because I was able to add cloud research, platform comparisons, recommendations, Linux investigations, screenshots, and reflections. And through these activities, I improved my skills in Linux commands, Markdown, GitHub, research, and technical documentation. Overall, the mission helped me understand how cloud platforms can be matched with real-world business needs.
